HOW TO FIGHT CHAIN - BAR QTE

This recreates the bar struggle from Roblox CHAIN final fight.

  1. Click PLAY. Chain grabs you.
  2. A bar appears with a RANDOM LETTER A-Z to spam.
  3. SPAM that letter as fast as you can - each press fills the bar a little.
  4. The bar constantly drains by itself. If it hits 0%, you fail.
  5. While you spam, the screen shakes violently and the bar moves/drifts around (configurable).
  6. Survive for 4.0s to complete one round.
  7. Rest briefly, then next round starts with a new letter.
  8. Survive 10 rounds to escape Chain.

Chain tips: Use two fingers alternating on same key for faster spam. On CHAIN difficulty, drain is brutal.

Only A-Z counts. Wrong key = small penalty. Spam = life.
