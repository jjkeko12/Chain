HOW TO FIGHT CHAIN

This recreates the final fight from Roblox CHAIN.

  1. Click PLAY to enter the arena.
  2. A RANDOM LETTER A-Z will appear with a shrinking circle.
  3. Press that letter on your keyboard before time runs out.
  4. Success damages CHAIN. Failure damages YOU.
  5. Shake: The prompt violently shakes - intensity is configurable.
  6. Moving: The prompt drifts around the arena - intensity is configurable.
  7. Survive 10 rounds to win.

Tip: Higher shake + moving = closer to real Chain experience. Try CHAIN difficulty.

Keys: Only A-Z accepted. Space and symbols are ignored.
